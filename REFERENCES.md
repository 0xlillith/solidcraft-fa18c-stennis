These two documents preserve the first showcase model's historical source trail, including the circa 2009–2010 Stennis / CVW-9 / VFA-146 setting and the principal NG301 launch photograph. They are explicitly marked as historical notes. Current geometry, materials, mechanism behavior, and RC3 compatibility are described by the current documents and validation records. Adding the inherited notes makes no change to model geometry.

## Scope of the reconstruction

Published dimensions, replica measurements, visible features, and broad equipment organization guide the scene. Exact internal stations, blade profiles, wall gauges, detailed wire/pipe routes, historical equipment offsets, and some small linkage geometry are authored estimates. Public -400-family engine information is distinguished from -402-specific facts. OpenHornet measurements describe replica components rather than aircraft production drawings.

The model does not embed DCS/OpenHornet geometry, textures, screenshots, CAD paths, or artwork. Reference photographs are not used as surface textures. Contributors and organizations are credited as sources, without an endorsement claim.
